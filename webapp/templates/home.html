Gene Similarity Search

GeneSim compares a protein or nucleotide query sequence with more than 18,000 human genes using four similarity measures: local alignment, k-mer composition, edit distance and promoter motif similarity. Each measure is reported on its own scale.

Start searching

Similarity methods

No single score captures everything that makes two genes alike. GeneSim runs the measures you choose side by side, keeps each on its own scale, and lets you rank by the one that answers your question.

Protein or nucleotide. Every metric works on amino-acid and DNA sequences, each with scoring suited to the molecule.

BLOSUM62 and DNA alignment

Local alignment that rewards conserved regions. Proteins are scored with the BLOSUM62 substitution matrix and BLAST-style gap costs; nucleotides with match, mismatch and gap scores.

Sequence conservationProteinNucleotide

K-mer similarity

Breaks each sequence into short overlapping words, three letters for protein and seven for DNA, and measures the share they have in common. It needs no alignment, so GeneSim also uses it to narrow down candidates.

Shared compositionAlignment-freeJaccard index

Edit distance

Counts the insertions, deletions and substitutions needed to turn one sequence into the other, normalised by length. Simple, strict, and easy to interpret.

Character-level differenceLevenshtein

JASPAR motif similarity

Compares the transcription factor binding sites found in each gene's promoter, using JASPAR 2024 profiles. It can connect genes that are regulated alike even when their sequences differ.

Shared regulationPromoter regionsJASPAR 2024
