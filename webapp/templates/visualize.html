Reading these charts

Alignment rewards conserved regions and is the most biologically faithful sequence metric.

K-mer counts shared short words. Its values run lower than alignment's, but its ranking often agrees.

Edit distance penalises every insertion, deletion and substitution, so length differences pull it down.

Motif compares promoter regulation, not the sequence itself. A match that scores high here but low on alignment may be co-regulated rather than related in sequence.
