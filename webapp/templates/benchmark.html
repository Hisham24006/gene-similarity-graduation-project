How to read this
Speed (avg time) Time for a single pairwise comparison. K-mer and Motif are near-instant. BLOSUM62 is much slower, especially on long sequences like BRCA2 (3,418 AA).
Agreement (r) Pearson correlation with BLOSUM62 scores. High r = agrees with BLOSUM62. Motif can disagree — this is intentional, revealing functional similarity that sequence alone misses.
