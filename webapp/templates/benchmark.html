How to read this
Speed, average time

The time for a single pairwise comparison. K-mer and motif are near-instant: motif compares cached transcription factor sets. BLOSUM62 and edit distance are much slower, especially on long proteins such as BRCA2 (3,418 aa).

Agreement, r

Pearson correlation with BLOSUM62 scores. A high r means the metric ranks pairs much as alignment does. Motif can disagree on purpose: it reveals shared regulation that sequence similarity alone misses.
